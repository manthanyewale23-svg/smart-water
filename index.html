Municipal Water Balance Formula

Water Loss (Unaccounted for Water) is calculated continuously as: Loss = Total Bulk Supply (L) − Total Billed Consumption (L)

🟢 LOW LOSS (< 10%)

Standard acceptable distribution loss due to measurement margins.

🟡 MEDIUM LOSS (10% – 20%)

Requires acoustic pipe inspection and pressure regulation balancing.

🔴 HIGH LOSS (> 20%)

Major pipe rupture, illegal siphoning, or tank overflow. Immediate maintenance dispatched.
